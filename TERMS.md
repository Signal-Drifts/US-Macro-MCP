## Interpreting results

Check each observation's date, units, frequency and source. Economic data may be
delayed, revised, missing or incorrect, and service availability is not guaranteed.
Percent changes calculated from a seasonally adjusted index may differ from a
publisher's headline calculation or rounding. Recession signal thresholds are
descriptive historical indicators, not forecasts. Outputs are for information
and research, not individualized investment advice.
